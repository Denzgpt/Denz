Rules
  • Playbook account (from your Trading Playbook): buy only inside a gate, at 2.5:1 or better to target; risk 1% of equity per trade, starter cap 1.5%, single-name cap 10%; trailing stop once +15%; cut at −25% from cost; no entries within 24 hours of a holding's earnings; no new entries after a −3% day; half size at −8% drawdown, none at −12%. No re-buy for a week after a stop-out. Gates for PANW, META and CRWD are from 19 Oct and need your refresh.
  • Max-ROI account: long-only trend pullback. Buys a stock in an uptrend (50-day above 200-day) that has pulled back 1.5 ATR from its 20-day high, only while SPY is above its 50-day average. Stop 2 ATR, target 4 ATR, 15-day time stop, risk 1%, 15% single-name cap, same drawdown guards.
  • Swarm can veto any entry for a name or pause everything. It cannot place a trade.
  • Hours: Swiss (SIX) and US sessions, 08:00 to 21:00 UTC on weekdays. The account is in USD; Swiss stocks are converted from CHF at the live rate. Fills: 5-minute bars, next bar after a signal, 3 bp slippage each way, $1 per order. Paper money only; nothing touches IBKR.
